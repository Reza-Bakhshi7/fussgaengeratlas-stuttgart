# BFRK infrastructure exports

The author supplied four semicolon-separated CSV exports: BFRK_Aufzug.csv, BFRK_Rampe.csv, BFRK_Rolltreppe.csv and BFRK_Treppe.csv. They describe station/accessibility infrastructure, including facility identifiers, coordinates, attributes, survey dates and public photograph links.

Source: MobiData BW / NVBW, https://mobidata-bw.de/dataset/daten-barrierefreiheit . The official profile specifies Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung – Version 2.0, https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/by-2-0 . Atlas changes include selecting Stuttgart facilities, adapting fields and classification/rendering. Survey dates in rows are not proof of the export/download date. Exact historical export timing remains to record. Photograph target contents have not been independently audited or archived.
